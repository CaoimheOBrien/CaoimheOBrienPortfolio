Behind This Project

These are some backgrounds that I made for my Dingle Animation Game Sting 2026 I created 2 of the 5 locations for this game.

This background depicts An Phiast's cave - a moody, atmospheric cavern used in the sting. This is where the characters have their "mission board", which holds their progress in the game.

Key choices: Lucious green grass and foilage surrounding the gorgeous blue lake.

Behind This Project

This is Lili Lasourris, a character that I made as concept art for a short film, "Lasourris Très Bizarre", which translates to "Very Strange Lasourris". It is a short film set in Marseilles, France which follows Lili, a young girl who is trying her best to navigate the complexities of her daily life.

Lili is a shy, young girl who does not have any whiskers, unlike the other rodents, this makes it hard for people to understand what she's feeling sometimes, she also finds it hard to read other peoples whiskers. She is the youngest of three, with her siblings having moved to the city, but Lili has committed herself to the quiet life.

She is a curious and determined girl who faces various challenges throughout the film as she is not like the other rodents in her village. This short film would explore these of growing up with a neurodivergency, family, culture and rural life.

Lasourris is a play on the words "La Souris" which means "the mouse" in French.

Behind This Project

These are the storyboards for a 2D animatic I made for one of my class in university using Adobe Animate. The animatic is about a character's morning routine and the storyboards were created to plan out the different scenes and shots.

Our class was told to create a storyboard for "something" and so I decided to put together my morning routine, to reflect on how I take certain, seemingly mundane, aspects of my day for granted. It was also an early morning class so, my morning routine was still on my mind.

Once I had chosen an idea, I wrote a basic script and colour coded it. Upon finishing the script, I created rough thumbnails for each of the shots. I paid attention to the composition and framing of each panel to ensure that it would be visually appealing and effective in conveying the story.

Using the thumbnails I created an in-depth story board using Adobe Photoshop. I chose not to use colour in my animatic as Ireland most often has dull, foggy mornings. Once I finished the panels I put them together in Adobe Animate and After Effects to add sound and other sound effects that I found on youtube/ recorded myself. All SFX are not copyrighted and are free to use. The vocals were recorded by my classmate; Liam Treacy.

Enemy Animation

I created this 2D animation as part of a Concept Art for Games module in Semester 1 of Year 2 of my degree.

The animation was created using a pixelated art style to give it a retro feel. I used a limited color palette to create a cohesive look and feel for the animation.

I started by sketching out the basic design for the enemy character. I then created a series of frames for the animation using a software called Aesprite, focusing on the movement and actions of the character.

I used a combination of traditional animation techniques and digital tools to create the animation. I made sure to pay attention to the timing and spacing of the animation to ensure that it was smooth and fluid, especially since it was going ot be used in a game.

Overall, I am very happy with how this animation turned out. It showcases my skills as an animator and my ability to create engaging and visually appealing content.

Sage Academy

Here is the link to the live page for this website: Sage Academy Live link

Here is the link to the GitHub repo: GitHub Link

This website was created as a group project for my Web UI Design module in Semester 2 of Year 2 of my degree.

Our group was tasked with creating a website for a fictional educational institution called "Sage Academy". The website was to be designed to attract potential students and provide information about the courses and programs offered by the academy.

As a group, we started by researching other educational websites to get an idea of what worked well and what didn't. We also brainstormed ideas for the layout and design of the website.

Once we had a solid plan in place, we divided the work among the group members. I moved towards a leadership role in this project, as I had more experience with web design and development than some of the other group members. I took on the role of project manager, ensuring that everyone was on track and that the project was progressing smoothly.

We used PenPot to create wireframes and prototypes for the website. This allowed us to visualize the layout and design of the website before we started coding.

I was tasked with creating the sign-in, contact us, and quizzes pages for the website. I also created several components that were used throughout the site, such as buttons, cards and forms.

We used a combination of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript to create the website. We also made sure to optimize the website for mobile devices, as we knew that many potential students would be accessing the site from their phones.

Overall, I am very proud of the work that our group did on this project. The website turned out great, and I learned a lot about working through conflict resolution and collaboration in a team setting.

CoffeeUtopia

I created this 2D game as part of a UI prototyping module in Semester 1 of Year 2 of my degree. Our prompt was to make a game based on the theme of "Utopia" and I felt that my perfect utopia was a nice cozy coffee shop.

I first created all the assets for this game in Aesprite, using a pixelated art style to give it a retro feel. I used a limited color palette to create a cohesive look and feel for the game as I watned it to have a "cozy" vibe.

I then used HTML, CSS, and JavaScript to create the game. This was the first time I had used the canvas function in HTML so it was really fun to explore this because it was like I was coding in C++ which I hadd done in my first year of university.

I made sure to pay attention to the user experience and interface design to ensure that the game was intuitive; just in case a user got confused there is a list of instructions in the top left corner of the game screen.

Unlike my other projects, this was hosted on codeberg which is an alternative to GitHub, however I prefer GitHub as it is easier to launch pages and more widely used.

Overall, I am very happy with how this game turned out. It showcases my skills as a game designer and my ability to create engaging and visually appealing content.

Optometry Poster

This is a poster that I worked on in my personal time. I worked with an optometrist to create a poster to highlight certain statistics about eye health that they had recently written a paper on. He wanted an aide for his presentation to the Eurpopean Union of Optometry.

In addition to working with the author on the design and typography of the poster for display I also assisted with the data analysis and am named co-author of this academic peer-reviewed publication which was published / displayed at the 2025 European Academy of Optometry & Optics in Ljubljana.

I used Adobe Photoshop to create the poster, focusing on a clean and professional design. I used a combination of images and text to create a visually appealing poster that would catch the attention of potential patients.

I made sure to pay attention to the typography and layout of the poster to ensure that it was easy to read and understand. I used a color scheme that was consistent with the optometrist's branding, using blues and greens to create a calming and professional look as well as ensuring that it could be read by people with a variety of different levels of colour blindess.

Overall, I am very happy with how this poster turned out. It showcases my skills as a graphic designer and my ability to create engaging and visually appealing content. I am also very proud to have co-authored this publication and to have contributed to the field of optometry in this way.
